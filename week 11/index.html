SeoulMate

Explore Seoul with people who know it. SeoulMate brings local guides, small-group tours, and flexible day planning together to help visitors spend less time navigating and more time discovering the city.

Open the live SeoulMate app ↗ Return to coursework

The idea

A more personal way to explore Seoul

SeoulMate is a travel-guide project for visitors who want help discovering the city. It connects travelers with local guides and group experiences, while giving independent visitors tools and information to plan their own days.

What you can do

Main features

Join a group tour

Browse upcoming small-group walks and experiences, including details such as meeting points, duration, available seats, and who is joining.

Find a local guide

Explore guide profiles by specialty and language, review ratings, and discover private-tour options.

Plan a day in Seoul

Choose places to visit and use the day planner to organize an itinerary across the city.

Explore practical city information

Find guides to places, food, hotels, and transport, along with a Seoul weather forecast and interactive map.

Project details
