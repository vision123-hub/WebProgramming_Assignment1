Simple Pasta Recipe

What You Need

  • 200g pasta
  • 1 cup tomato sauce
  • 1 small onion
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  1. Boil water in a large pot and add some salt.
  2. Add the pasta and cook it until it becomes soft.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan and cook the chopped onion and garlic.
  4. Add the tomato sauce and season it with salt and pepper.
  5. Be careful when handling the hot pan and boiling water.
  6. Mix the cooked pasta with the sauce and serve it while it is warm.
