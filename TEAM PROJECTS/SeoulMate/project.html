SeoulMate

A travel companion for discovering Seoul with local guides, small-group experiences, and a flexible day planner. SeoulMate brings these tools together to help visitors spend less time navigating and more time exploring.

Visit the live SeoulMate app ↗ Return to coursework

01 · Introduction

A more connected way to explore Seoul

SeoulMate is a web travel guide for visitors who want help discovering the city. Its purpose is to bring trip planning, local knowledge, and group experiences together in one place, whether someone wants to explore with a guide or plan a day independently.

02 · Problem and solution

From travel planning to city exploring

The problem

Visitors may have to piece together places to visit, route planning, local guidance, and group activities across different services. Navigating an unfamiliar city and finding experiences that fit their language and interests can add friction to a trip.

The solution

SeoulMate brings local guide discovery, group tours, day planning, and practical city information into one travel companion, with options for guided and independent exploration.

03 · Platform features

Ways to explore Seoul

Group tours

Browse upcoming small-group experiences, see who is joining, and find tour details before booking.

Local guides

Explore guide profiles with languages, specialties, and traveller reviews, and find options for private guiding.

Day planner

Choose places to visit and organize a day with suggested stop order and walking or subway travel times.

Practical travel information

Check Seoul weather and browse city guidance. The site offers English and Korean language options.

04 · Development

Technologies and services

Verified in the deployed project

  • Next.js — the deployed application serves Next.js-generated assets.
  • Stripe — used for checkout; the application notes that payment details go directly to Stripe.
  • Open-Meteo — credited as the weather data provider.
  • Vercel — the live application is deployed at a Vercel domain.
